Citizen Safety Guide: Prevention & What You Should Do

Essential precautions to protect yourself and family from financial fraud and fake schemes

🚫 STRICTLY AVOID

What NOT To Do (Prevention)

  • ❌
    Never Pay Any Fee: Genuine government schemes NEVER ask for registration, processing, stamp, or courier fees.
  • ❌
    Never Share OTP or Bank Info: Never share Aadhaar OTP, bank account numbers, or UPI PINs.
  • ❌
    Do Not Click Unofficial Links: Avoid domains ending in .online, .xyz, .top, .site, or downloading third-party APKs.
  • ❌
    Do Not Forward Unverified Messages: Break the misinformation chain on WhatsApp and Telegram groups.
✅ RECOMMENDED ACTIONS

What You SHOULD Do

  • 🌐
    Cross-Check on .gov.in: Only verify through official portals ending with .gov.in or .nic.in.
  • 🏛️
    Search on myScheme.gov.in: Verify real eligibility criteria on the Central Scheme Portal.
  • 📞
    Report Fraud Immediately: If you lost money, dial National Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 or register at cybercrime.gov.in.
  • 📢
    Warn the Sender: Inform the person who sent this forward that it is an audited fake scam.
